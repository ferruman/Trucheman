# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Trucheman a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Welcome questions and differing viewpoints.
- Give and accept actionable feedback gracefully.
- Respect privacy and do not publish credentials, personal data, or copyrighted book content.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, trolling, and publishing another person's
private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the maintainer using the contact
information on <https://github.com/ferruman>. Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly. The
maintainer may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently ban participants whose
behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is officially representing the project.
